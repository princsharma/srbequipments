FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our truck & trailer repair services. Don't see your question? Call us anytime.

Talk to a Technician
Can I get same-day repair service for my heavy-duty truck?

Yes. We offer same-day truck repair service, both in-shop and on-site. Our certified technicians use the best diagnostic tools to reduce downtime and get your truck back on the road quickly in Edmonton.

Do you offer mobile service for both truck and trailer repairs?

Absolutely. Our fully equipped mobile service unit handles both truck and trailer repairs at your yard or roadside throughout the Edmonton area and beyond.

What routine truck service can you perform at my facility?

We perform oil changes, brake service, DOT/CVIP inspections, fluid top-ups, lighting, tire checks, suspension, and minor mechanical repairs at your facility.

Do you provide emergency repairs for cargo trucks?

Yes — we provide 24/7 emergency repair service for cargo trucks, semi-trucks, and trailers. Call (780) 878-5221 for immediate dispatch.

Do you offer 24/7 mobile truck repair in Edmonton?

Yes. Our mobile truck repair service is available 24/7 across Edmonton and surrounding regions for fleet customers and one-time emergencies.

Can you perform CVIP inspections in Edmonton?

Yes. We are a licensed CVIP inspection facility serving Edmonton and surrounding areas. We inspect trucks, trailers, and buses in line with Alberta provincial safety standards.

Are your diesel truck mechanics certified?

All of our diesel truck mechanics are Red Seal certified with extensive heavy-duty experience on Class 8 trucks and trailers.

Can you perform a full engine overhaul?

Yes. We perform full engine overhauls and in-frame rebuilds on most major diesel truck engines — CAT, Cummins, Detroit, Volvo, and more.

What areas do you service around Edmonton?

We service Edmonton, Sherwood Park, Leduc, Nisku, Spruce Grove, St. Albert, Fort Saskatchewan, Acheson, and surrounding Alberta communities.

What are common signs my truck needs immediate repair?

Warning lights, loss of power, unusual noises, smoke, brake issues, leaks, overheating, or abnormal vibrations — these all warrant immediate inspection.

How long does a typical truck or trailer repair take?

Most routine repairs are completed the same day. Larger jobs like engine overhauls or full transmissions vary depending on parts availability. We'll give you a clear ETA after diagnostics.
